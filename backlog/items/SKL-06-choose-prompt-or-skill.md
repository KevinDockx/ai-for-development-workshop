## Story or problem statement

Take a prompt you repeat and decide whether it belongs in a prompt file or a skill. Make the choice based on your actual client and record why. Prompt files remain a Local-harness option but are deprecated for Agent Host; skills are the repository option under `.github/skills`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A task you actually repeat is identified.
- [ ] The actual client and execution mode are recorded.
- [ ] Prompt-file and skill behavior are compared for that client.
- [ ] One option is implemented and the decision is justified.
- [ ] The result works in the selected client.

## Notes

Do not choose from habit. Client support and the amount of reusable procedure both matter.

## AI approach hint

Ask AI to compare discoverability, portability, and execution needs for this one repeated task.
