## Story or problem statement

Replace the stale claim reopening specification with a current draft that has an owner, a status, and unambiguous behavior for reopening a closed claim when new evidence arrives.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The old specification is clearly retired or superseded.
- [ ] The replacement has an owner and `draft` status.
- [ ] The 90-day rule, eligible claim state, permissions, audit needs, and new-evidence requirement are explicit.
- [ ] Open questions are marked as open rather than invented.
- [ ] The specification links to FEA-01.

## Notes

Use `claim-reopening-v0.md` as historical input only. Reopening is not currently supported.
