## Story or problem statement

Create an ADR writer agent, or adopt and adapt a suitable one found online. It must preserve evidence, alternatives, status, and consequences rather than turning every discussion into an accepted decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The agent is stored under `.github/agents`.
- [ ] Adopted content has been reviewed before use.
- [ ] The output format supports proposed, accepted, rejected, and superseded decisions.
- [ ] A trial ADR cites its source evidence.
- [ ] The trial records at least one real consequence or trade-off.

## Notes

If you adopt an agent, record its source and your changes.

## AI approach hint

Test the agent on a meeting note where the decision is only implicit.
